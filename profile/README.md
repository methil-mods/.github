# Methil Game Development Collective

Welcome to the official GitHub repository of Methil, a group of developers who love creating things just for the fun of it!

## About Methil

Methil is a collective of developers who enjoy making cool, fun, and sometimes silly games. We’re all about experimenting, learning, and having a great time while bringing our ideas to life.

## Our Mission

At Methil, our goals are pretty simple:

- Have fun making games  
- Create unique and entertaining experiences  
- Share our projects with the world  
- Learn new skills and grow as developers  

We’re not trying to change the world—just make some fun games and enjoy the process!

## Getting Involved

If you’re into game development and like what we’re about, we’d love for you to join us!

1. **Check out our games**: See what we’ve been working on  
2. **Share your ideas**: Got a cool game concept? Hit us up!  
3. **Join the team**: Whether you code, design, draw, or just love games, come create with us!  

Thanks for stopping by! Let’s make awesome games together!
